House rules

  1. Cleaning day is Wednesday. Your job is done by 10pm, all boxes ticked.
  2. Trash goes to the curb Wednesday night and the bins come back in Thursday.
  3. Only tick what you actually did. If you skip a step, leave it unticked and say why in the group chat.
  4. Swaps are fine if both people agree in the group chat first. Whoever does the job ticks the boxes.
  5. Daily mess is on whoever made it: dishes within 24 hours, wipe the stove and counter after cooking.
  6. Sponges, dish towels and trash bags get replaced by whoever notices they need it.
  7. Missed job with no swap: it has to be done by the next day at the latest.
